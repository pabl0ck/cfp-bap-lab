\noindent\textbf{Data and code availability}\quad
The experiment scripts, instance-generator seeds, raw per-instance results,
and convergence histories underlying Tables~1 and~2 and the numerical
figures are available in the versioned reproducibility package
\href{https://github.com/pabl0ck/cfp-bap-lab/tree/eccrm-repro-v1}
{\texttt{cfp-bap-lab}, tag \texttt{eccrm-repro-v1}}.
The package includes pinned dependencies, the measured configurations and
environment, and a postprocessing script that regenerates the tables,
paired statistics, and figures directly from the archived measurements.
